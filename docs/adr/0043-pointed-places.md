# Pointed places stay on the message

A building, road, pipe, cable, or ground point the player marks becomes a card on that chat message, plus a short place list in the text the Agent reads. The city does not draw the mark. Clicking the card moves the camera. A ground point always can, because the coordinates remain. A building or road can while that object still exists; after it is gone the card keeps its name, and the click tells the player it cannot be found.

The mark is not a model tool, not a pin or temporary prop in the city, and not a change to the system prompt on later turns. Sending includes the current cards in that message and clears the unsent ones. The card's dismiss control removes one before send.
