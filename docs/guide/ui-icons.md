# UI icons

The launcher is the AIRI illustration, shown as an image inside the game's floating button. The button is the rounded plate. Its fill is the character pink, not the toolbar blue: closed `#DD5E8E`, open `#E37BA2`, and the press `#FAF9FB`. The illustration has no plate of its own.

Character fills stay `#EC92AD`, `#F3E2E7`, and `#FFF4F4`. Colors are literals. This Gameface build drops `var()` in mod stylesheets.

Send, stop, and the tool-row chevron are Solar Bold Duotone. Both layers use `currentColor`; the lighter layer is the icon's own 50% opacity.
